Email **support@ironmile.dev**. We reply by email.

## What to include

- Your Atlassian site name (for example `yourcompany.atlassian.net`) and the Jira Cloud and Jira Service Management product.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps.
- The app version if you know it.
- Tell us the file type (`.msg` or `.eml`), its size, and the message the panel shows. Attachments over 25 MiB are not opened in the panel; Jira's own download still works.

## What not to send

Please do not send email content or attachments you consider sensitive. We only receive what you email us, and we delete it on request.
