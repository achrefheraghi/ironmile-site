# Support

## Get help

Email **[support@ironmile.dev](mailto:support@ironmile.dev)**, or open an issue at [github.com/achrefheraghi/pleading-paper/issues](https://github.com/achrefheraghi/pleading-paper/issues). Describe what you did, what you expected and what happened. **Do not paste case details or other private information.** Issues are public; email is private.

## Quick start

1. Open a Google Docs™ document.
2. Choose **Extensions > Pleading Paper & Caption Builder > Set up pleading paper**. Line numbers 1 to 28 and the rules appear on every page.
3. Choose **Build caption…**, fill in the sidebar and click **Insert caption**. The caption goes at the top of the document, and the footer title is added.
4. To undo everything, choose **Remove pleading paper**. Your text is kept.

## Common questions

**The text does not line up with the numbers.** Run **Set up pleading paper** again. It resets every body paragraph to 12 pt Times New Roman with the matching line spacing and no extra space before or after.

**I clicked Build caption twice.** The second caption replaces the first; it does not add a copy.

**My footer already has a page number or firm name.** The add-on keeps it and adds the rule and title below it. If the footer gets too tall for the page, the sidebar shows a note.

**Does it meet my court's rules?** It follows the common California 28-line layout. Check your court's local rules before filing; see the [terms](https://ironmile.dev/pleading-paper/terms).

**What data does it collect?** None. See the [privacy policy](https://ironmile.dev/pleading-paper/privacy).
