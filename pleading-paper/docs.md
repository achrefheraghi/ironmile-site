Pleading Paper & Caption Builder is an editor add-on for Google Docs™. It does two jobs: it sets up 28-line pleading paper, and it builds a California-style court caption. It runs only inside the document you have open.

This add-on is a formatting tool, not legal advice. Court rules differ and change. Check your court's local rules and, for California state courts, the California Rules of Court, rules 2.100 to 2.119, before you file.

## What you need

- A Google account and a document in Google Docs™.
- The add-on installed from the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/pleading_paper_caption_builder_for_googl/567104884525).

## Install

1. Open the [Marketplace listing](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/pleading_paper_caption_builder_for_googl/567104884525).
2. Select **Install** and accept the two permissions Google asks for: "View and manage documents that this application has been installed in" and "Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications". The second one lets the add-on show its sidebar and help window. The add-on has no access to your Google Drive™, makes no calls to outside servers and collects no data.
3. Open a document. The add-on appears under **Extensions > Pleading Paper & Caption Builder**.

## Set up pleading paper

1. Open the document, then choose **Extensions > Pleading Paper & Caption Builder > Set up pleading paper**.
2. The add-on sets the page to US Letter with pleading margins, formats the body text (Times New Roman 12 pt) so each line sits beside a number, and adds 28 line numbers with a double rule on the left and a single rule on the right.
3. Running it again never duplicates anything.

**Page numbers.** Google Docs™ does not let add-ons insert page numbers. Choose **Insert > Page numbers**, then place them in the footer, above the rule.

## Build a caption

1. Choose **Extensions > Pleading Paper & Caption Builder > Build caption...**. A form opens in the sidebar.
2. Fill in the form. The fields are:
   - **Filer:** name, address, city, state and ZIP (required); State Bar number, phone and email (optional); "Attorney or party for" (Plaintiff, Defendant, Petitioner or Respondent); client name, if you are the attorney
   - **Court:** court name (filled in as SUPERIOR COURT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA; change it if needed) and county (required)
   - **Parties:** plaintiff(s) and defendant(s), each with a label such as Plaintiff, Plaintiffs, Petitioner, Defendant or Respondent
   - **Document:** document title (required) and case number
   - **Hearing and dates (all optional):** date, time, department, judge, action filed and trial date
   - **Options:** "Add the document title to the footer", with an optional shorter footer title, and "Put the court name on line 9 (strict local rules)"
3. Select **Insert caption**. The caption is inserted at the top of the document.

If you are self-represented, leave the State Bar number blank and the caption reads "In Pro Per".

Inserting again replaces the caption instead of adding a second one. Text you typed into the old caption is kept, so delete any leftover lines yourself if you no longer need them. An existing footer is never replaced: the rule and the document title are added below its content.

## Remove pleading paper

Choose **Extensions > Pleading Paper & Caption Builder > Remove pleading paper**. This removes the line numbers, rules and the footer rule and title the add-on created. Your text, page numbers, the caption, any other footer lines and the page format stay as they are.

## Troubleshooting

**Page 1 has no line numbers.** The line numbers and rules live in the document's default header. If **Different first page** is on (**Format > Headers & footers**), page 1 uses another header. Turn it off.

**The numbers do not line up with the text.** Keep the header and footer margins at the default 0.5 inch (**File > Page setup**). Changing them moves the line numbers or squeezes the body.

**The caption does not fit on page 1.** Shorten the document title or the party names.

**There are no page numbers.** Add them yourself with **Insert > Page numbers**, in the footer above the rule.
