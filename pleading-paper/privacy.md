# Privacy policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

This policy covers the Google Docs™ add-on "Pleading Paper & Caption Builder" (the add-on).

## What the add-on can access

The add-on asks Google for two permissions:

- **View and manage documents that this application has been installed in** (`documents.currentonly`). The add-on reads and changes only the document you have open when you use it. It cannot open your other documents or your Google Drive.
- **Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications** (`script.container.ui`). This lets the add-on show its sidebar and help window.

## What the add-on does with your document

When you choose a menu item, the add-on:

- adds or removes the line numbers, rules and footer that make up pleading paper;
- inserts or replaces the caption from the details you type in the sidebar.

The details you type (names, addresses, case number and similar) go into your document and nowhere else.

## What the add-on does not do

- It stores nothing outside your document. It keeps no copy of your text or caption details.
- It makes no network calls to the developer or to anyone else. It has no server.
- It collects no analytics, uses no cookies and shows no ads.
- It does not sell, share or transfer your data, and it does not use it to train AI models.

## Error logs

If the add-on hits an unexpected error, Google Apps Script may record the error message in the developer's Google Cloud logs, as it does for any Apps Script project. These messages describe the failed step (for example "Caption table has no rows"). They do not contain your document text. Google keeps them for a limited time under its own retention rules.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

The add-on's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

You can uninstall the add-on from Extensions > Add-ons > Manage add-ons in Google Docs. You can revoke its permissions at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). The pleading paper and caption the add-on added stay in your document until you remove them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
