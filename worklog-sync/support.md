Email **support@ironmile.dev**. We reply by email.

## What to include

- Your Atlassian site name (for example `yourcompany.atlassian.net`) and the Jira Cloud product.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps.
- The app version if you know it.

- To disconnect your own Harvest account, use "My Harvest connection" in the app. Disconnecting deletes your stored token and pauses your sync.

## What not to send

Please do not send your Harvest token or any other secret. We only receive what you email us, and we delete it on request.
