Email **support@ironmile.dev**. We reply by email.

## What to include

- Your Atlassian site name (for example `yourcompany.atlassian.net`) and the Confluence Cloud product.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot helps.
- The app version if you know it.

- Import or export problems: tell us the space size and whether it happens on export to PDF or Word, or in the admin import scan.

## What not to send

Please do not send code or page content you consider sensitive. We only receive what you email us, and we delete it on request.
